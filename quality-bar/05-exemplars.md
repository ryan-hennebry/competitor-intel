The author reads this file **last**, immediately before writing. Each entry is a sentence, the move
it makes, and one line on why it works. **Calibrate on the move, never on the nouns.** Every
sentence below came out of one delivered brief, in that brief's market and in that market's
vocabulary; what carries to the next brief is the shape, never a word of the subject matter. A
citation reads `<brief> :n`, meaning line n of that brief's markdown.

## Whole-paragraph gold

Calibrate on density and flow, never on the punchlines. The two are from different markets on
purpose.

<!-- illustrative -->
- **Round Quick Take ¶1** (Round :14): "**Not one of the six says where its agent stops.** All six
  sell agents that touch money, and all six bound them. Four rivals state a human gate in their own
  words." Evidence carried in plain sentences, one verdict, nothing bolted on.
- **Northwind final, Voltalis channel ¶:** "**Voltalis' GB home volume arrives through your channel, and
  its direct customers are buildings.** Powervault, 11 September 2025, runs cloud to cloud into
  Powervault's SMARTSTOR, "now accessible under the P415 grid code", across about 300 residential
  units, and there the household is paid. The Electric Heating Company, about 1,100 homes, and
  Passiv, 10,000 heat pumps and still prospective, are the same channel without the cash." These
  are counterparties with unit numbers. The red-team said: "I did not have that." There is no short
  sentence anywhere in it, and it does not need one.
